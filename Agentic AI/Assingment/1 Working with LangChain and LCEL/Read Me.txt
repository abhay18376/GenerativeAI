You have to create .env file and enter
OPENAI_API_KEY='your api key'

1. Upload a Service Agreement
	click upload the file and select service agreement
	Enter 'What are the terms for termination?' in left pane
	click Analyze Legal Document

2. Upload a Non Disclosure Agreement
	click upload the file and select non_disclosure_agreement
	Enter 'What is the duration of the confidentiality obligation?' in left pane
	click Analyze Legal Document

3. Generate a summary for a licensing contract to understand key obligations and payment structures
	Enter 'Generate a summary for a licensing contract to understand key obligations and payment structures' in left pane
	click Analyze Legal Query   --- dont upload any document
